Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from educators.

Does TCO automatically grade student exams without teacher involvement?

No. TCO operates under a strict 'AI assists. Teachers decide' model. The AI scans and extracts handwriting, checks steps against the approved marking scheme, and flags borderline answers for your explicit review. The final mark is only submitted when the teacher confirms it.

Can I speak or type in Hindi / Hinglish?

Yes! TCO's Master Agent naturally understands mixed English and Hinglish prompts like 'Class 12 ke 20 MCQ bana do' or 'Ye answer sheets check kar do', automatically determining syllabus requirements and formatting outputs accordingly.

Is student data used to train commercial AI models?

No. Student assignments, assessment grades, and personal identifying information are kept confidential and are never used to train public commercial AI models.
